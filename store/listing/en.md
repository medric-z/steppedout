## Product name

SteppedOut

## Short description

SteppedOut locks your PC when you walk away from it, the same lock you get with Win+L. It waits until you've really gone: music, video, calls and full-screen apps keep it unlocked, and a countdown in the corner lets you cancel first.

## Description

SteppedOut locks your PC when you haven't used the mouse or keyboard for the time you choose. It's the same lock you get by pressing Win+L, so your apps, downloads and anything else you left running carry on. Locking isn't sleep.

It knows when you haven't really left:
• While music or video is playing, it can wait longer or not lock at all.
• Any app using your microphone counts as a call.
• An app in full screen can hold off the lock.
• Using a controller counts as using the PC.

Before it locks, a countdown appears in the corner of the screen. Move the mouse or press a key to cancel it. Try it shows the countdown without locking, so you can see what it looks like.

When you're away, it can pause music and videos, mute the PC, turn off the screen, play a short sound, open or close the apps you choose, and run your own script. When you're back, it can show how long you were away, play music again, open apps and run a script. Pausing music and videos while you're away, playing them again when you're back, and showing how long you were away are on from the start, and you can turn them off. The rest are off until you turn them on. Pausing works for apps that show in Windows' media controls.

You can also:
• Use a different lock time when the laptop isn't plugged in.
• Lock sooner, later or never while a specific app is open.
• Turn on a keyboard shortcut, such as Ctrl+Alt+L, to lock right away from any app.
• Turn off the screen or put the PC to sleep when it locks.
• Pause it for a set time, up to 7 days, or until you turn it back on.
• Save your settings to a file and load them again.
• Get reminders to take a break and to rest your eyes (20-20-20).
• Choose light or dark mode, or follow Windows, and make the text bigger or smaller.
• See what's delaying or stopping the lock right now with "Why isn't the PC locking?".

SteppedOut lives in an icon next to the clock and works in English and Arabic. It's free and open source, needs no account, makes no network connections and collects nothing. Your settings stay in a file on your PC.
